# Final model: test-set evaluation

Generated by `python run.py`.

* Model: `random_forest+sigmoid` (random forest, sigmoid-calibrated), trained on the full training split
* Decision threshold: **0.352**, chosen on the training split from out-of-fold predictions to reach a recall of 0.90
* Test set: 71 patients (33 admitted to ICU), held out from every earlier step and used once, after the model and threshold were fixed

## Results

95% intervals from 2000 bootstrap resamples of the test patients.

| Metric | Test set | 95% interval | Cross-validation (training split) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ROC-AUC | 0.643 | 0.502 – 0.765 | see cv_report.md |
| PR-AUC | 0.592 | 0.441 – 0.767 | see cv_report.md |
| Brier score | 0.236 | 0.205 – 0.272 | see cv_report.md |
| Recall | 0.818 | 0.667 – 0.938 | 0.900 |
| Precision | 0.519 | 0.383 – 0.655 | 0.539 |
| Specificity | 0.342 | 0.200 – 0.500 | 0.340 |
| F1 | 0.635 | 0.507 – 0.739 | see cv_report.md |

Recall, precision, specificity and F1 are at the decision threshold. Cross-validation values at the threshold are means over the 10 CV repeats.

## Compared with an age-only baseline

The same test patients scored by `age_only`: logistic regression on the age group alone, trained on the same training split. This comparison was added after the final model's test results had been seen, so it is a check on the final model, not a model choice; the final model was not changed.

| Metric | Final model | age_only |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ROC-AUC | 0.643 | 0.723 |
| PR-AUC | 0.592 | 0.664 |
| Brier score | 0.236 | 0.214 |

ROC-AUC difference (final model minus baseline): -0.080, 95% interval -0.228 to +0.055 (paired bootstrap, 2000 resamples of the test patients).
